Start Here

Black Banner Company is a persistent D&D 2024 Westmarch-style campaign. Your character continues between contracts, earns Progress Points, Gold and Work Hours, gathers persistent crafting materials, builds a Bastion, trades with other players, and can eventually retire into an Acolyte legacy.

1. Join & Register

Join Discord, create your character, and make sure the website recognizes your Discord account.

2. Take Contracts

Use the Contract Calendar and Discord contract posts. Contract rank controls who can join and what rank of rewards/materials may appear.

3. Spend Downtime

Each workweek provides 40 Work Hours for crafting, harvesting, Odd Jobs, or full-week training.

4. Build Your Legacy

At D Rank / level 6, begin a Bastion. At level 12, the Acolyte system becomes available.

Character Creation

This is the starting point for every Black Banner character. Character creation uses official D&D Beyond content and the Black Banner manual ability-score system below.

Your character choices are permanent once approved. Stats, species, class and background are locked after approval unless staff specifically approves a change.

1. Join a Black Banner D&D Beyond Campaign

Join one of the available Black Banner Company campaign slots on D&D Beyond, then choose Create Character. Campaign space is limited, so inactive players may be asked to temporarily remove characters and rejoin when they need shared campaign content or level updates.

2. Character Sheet Setup

  • Set Character Privacy to Public so staff can review the sheet.
  • Use officially released content available on D&D Beyond.
  • Unearthed Arcana, playtest and preview content are not allowed.
  • Free League / Lord of the Rings content is not used.
  • Gunslinger is not allowed.
  • If an older option has an official 2024 replacement, use the current 2024 version.

3. Ability Scores — 80-Point Manual Build

On the D&D Beyond Abilities tab, select Manual/Rolled and enter the scores yourself.

Distribute exactly 80 points among STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS and CHA, with every score between 8 and 18. Then apply the ability-score increases from your Background.

Example: 18 + 16 + 14 + 12 + 10 + 10 = 80 before Background increases.

4. Choose a Species

Choose an approved officially released species available through D&D Beyond. If it has an updated 2024 version, use the current version.

5. Choose a Background

Choose an approved Background or configure an allowed Custom Background. A Custom Background may have a custom name and description and use the available proficiency/language choices.

A 2024 Background normally includes its Origin Feat automatically. If your approved Background does not, add one approved Origin Feat through Features & Traits → Manage Feats.

6. Choose a Class

Choose an officially released class available on D&D Beyond. If it has an updated 2024 version, use that version. Gunslinger is not allowed.

7. Starting Equipment

Use either starting-equipment option offered by the builder. Any unused starting Gold is use it or lose it; leftover character-creation Gold is not carried into the Black Banner economy.

8. Submit for Approval

Open the public view of your completed D&D Beyond character sheet, copy its URL, and post it in the server's Character Approval channel. The character is not approved until staff reviews it.

9. Discord Display Name

Update your Discord server nickname so your characters and levels are easy to identify.

Example: Ooxy [ Aranis 20 ] [ Nyxaris 8 ]

Character Lock, Death & Retirement

  • Once approved, stats, species, class and background are locked unless staff approves a change.
  • After reaching Level 6, you may retire a character you no longer enjoy and create a new one under the current creation rules.
  • If a character dies before Level 6, the replacement uses the same stat spread as the dead character.
  • Death allows 5% of the dead character's Gold to transfer to the replacement.
  • Retirement allows 10% of the retired character's Gold to transfer to the replacement.
  • No items, materials or magic items transfer under this basic replacement-character rule. Acolyte inheritance is a separate system.

Contracts & Calendar

Contracts are the core adventure loop. DMs post a rank, date/time, party needs and contract information. The website Contract Calendar displays times in the viewer's local time.

  • Join contracts appropriate to your rank; the normal signup band is ±1 rank.
  • Rewards are finalized by the DM and can include PP, Gold, Work Hours, loot, components and crafting materials.
  • Failure matters: a failed contract can award reduced or no Gold, PP, loot, components or materials depending on the result.
  • Level 20 characters can play in Legend League when the contract specifically allows it; Legend League play does not award normal progression rewards.
Use the Contract Calendar for scheduled contracts.

Character Sheet, Inventory & Transfers

The website Character Sheet is your persistent player hub. It is tied to your Discord login and stores the website-facing character profile used by automated systems.

  • View character information and persistent crafting-material inventory.
  • Materials earned by harvesting/crafting systems are added directly to the persistent inventory.
  • Direct transfers use server-side identity checks; players cannot choose another Discord ID to impersonate.
  • Recent transfers provide a record of incoming and outgoing material movement.

Work Hours

The standard Black Banner workweek is 40 Work Hours. Work Hours are the downtime currency used for production and downtime activities.

Flexible Activities

Harvesting and Odd Jobs can use 8, 16, 24, 32, or 40 hours. Each 8 hours normally creates one attempt.

40-Hour Harvest Bonus

Mining, logging and foraging use a ×2 quantity multiplier when the full 40-hour option is selected. A Natural 20 doubles that successful attempt again.

Training is different: a training week consumes the entire 40-hour workweek and cannot be split with crafting, harvesting or Odd Jobs.

Harvesting: Mining, Logging, Foraging & Hunting

Crafting System

Crafting is a persistent material-based system with 20 professions, rank-based recipes, Prime/Base materials, Work Hour costs, crafting DCs, facilities, automatic inventory validation/consumption and special craft outcomes.

Craft Results

Many professions use the standard pattern: Natural 20 gives the profession's bonus result; success completes the craft; failure consumes a portion of committed materials; Natural 1 can consume all committed materials. Exact outcomes are listed per profession.

Facilities

Higher-rank recipes can require a Workshop, Laboratory or Library. Workshop Order Assist can grant Advantage when its requirements are met.

Professions

Alchemist, Brewer, Calligraphy, Carpenter, Cobbler, Cooking, Glassblower, Jeweller, Leatherworker, Mason, Painter, Potter, Smith, Weaver, Woodcarver, Herbalism, Forgery, Disguise and Poisoner, plus interdisciplinary crafts and trapmaking.

Important Current Rules

  • 40-hour standard workweek.
  • Scrolls follow the class spell-list restriction; you must be able to legally use/scribe the spell under the current scroll rules.
  • Cooking can use eligible Meat, Herbs and Blood where the recipe/system allows it.
  • Exotic arrows choose the required metal/wood; the material determines the crafted result.
  • Equipment repair uses the current repair procedure and materials listed in the Crafting Guide.
The recipe catalogue is intentionally kept in one authoritative place. The Crafting Guide contains every recipe's exact materials, Work Hours, DC, facility requirement and effect. Open the full Crafting Guide.

Material Market, Buy Orders & Trading

The Material Market is the persistent player exchange for crafting materials and eligible custom goods.

  • Listings use the player's persistent inventory and Gold.
  • Players can browse by material/profession filters and trade with other members.
  • Buy Orders allow players to advertise materials/items they want to purchase.
  • Direct transfers and market actions are tied to the logged-in Discord identity.
  • Transaction/inventory changes are performed server-side rather than trusting a player-supplied Discord ID.

Silent Auctions

Auctions are sealed-bid player auctions integrated with the Black Banner Gold economy.

  • Duration: 3 days.
  • Silent bidding: the current high bid is hidden while the auction is active.
  • Escrow: Gold is held when a bid is placed. Raising your own bid charges only the difference.
  • Refunds: losing bid holds are refunded when the auction ends.
  • Ties: an exact tie does not replace the earlier high bidder; a new bid must exceed the current hidden high bid.
  • Seller restriction: you cannot bid on your own auction.
  • Buy It Now: when enabled, it ends the auction and refunds sealed bid holds.
  • Anti-snipe: a successful bid with 5 minutes or less remaining resets the auction to 12 hours remaining.

Bastions — Getting Started

A Bastion is your character’s personal base of operations in Black Banner. It is a persistent stronghold that grows with your character and can eventually contain rooms, hirelings, storage, crafting spaces, defenses, harvesting facilities, and special Bastion Orders.

You do not need to learn the entire Bastion system before starting. The website handles much of the tracking for you. Your first goal is simply to unlock your Bastion and build your first room.

When Can I Get a Bastion?

You unlock the Bastion system at D Rank / Level 6.

Once you reach Level 6, your first goal is:

Build your first Basic Facility. Once that facility is completed, your Bastion officially exists.

Step 1 — Open My Bastion

Go to Bastions of the Black → My Bastion on the Black Banner website.

Your My Bastion page is where you will manage the parts of the system that apply to your character, including:

  • Naming your Bastion.
  • Building and upgrading rooms.
  • Tracking construction progress.
  • Assigning and moving hirelings.
  • Managing storage and personnel.
  • Building Special Facilities as you unlock them.
  • Issuing available Bastion Orders.
  • Rolling and resolving your Bastion Turn.

Step 2 — Build Your First Room

All new Basic Facilities begin as Cramped.

First RoomCostConstruction TimeMap Area
Cramped Basic Facility250 GP3 Construction Turns4 squares

You do not need to make the “perfect” first-room choice. Basic Facilities are the foundation of the Bastion and can be expanded and upgraded later.

Step 3 — Understand Construction Turns

Construction Turns are not Work Hours.

Each time you receive your normal workweek reset, you also receive one Construction Turn. Construction does not spend your 40 Work Hours.

Example — Building Your First Room A Cramped Basic Facility needs 3 Construction Turns.

Week 1 → 1/3
Week 2 → 2/3
Week 3 → 3/3 — Construction Complete

A completed facility becomes available according to the normal Bastion timing shown on the website.

Step 4 — Grow the Bastion With Your Character

At C Rank / Level 9, you begin unlocking Special Facilities.

Special Facilities are the rooms that make your Bastion unique. Depending on what you build, your Bastion might become a crafting center, military stronghold, research facility, harvesting operation, business, temple, hideout, or something else entirely.

Examples of Bastion facilities include Workshop, Laboratory, Library, Barracks, Harvestarium, Storage, Kitchen, Parlor, Shop, and many others.

Step 5 — Hirelings

Some rooms use Hirelings to operate or unlock their full benefits.

  • If a newly built facility needs a hireling, its first required hireling is free.
  • Some room-size upgrades can provide additional free hirelings.
  • Additional hirelings can be purchased when the room has space for them.
  • Your website Bastion page lets you assign and move hirelings instead of keeping a separate manual list.

Step 6 — Bastion Orders

Many facilities can perform a special Order during a Bastion Turn.

An Order might allow a room to:

  • Craft or improve something.
  • Gather resources.
  • Research information.
  • Train characters or personnel.
  • Manage defenders or hirelings.
  • Generate another special facility benefit.

The available Orders depend on the facilities you actually own. Use the website and Bastion Guide rather than trying to memorize every Order in advance.

Step 7 — Roll Your Bastion Turn

When your Bastion Turn is available, use the My Bastion page.

The website can handle the Bastion Turn roll and display the result. Depending on the outcome, you may have an event, reward, construction update, or another choice to resolve.

Beginner Rule of Thumb Reach Level 6 → Open My Bastion → Name it → Build your first Basic Facility → Let construction finish → Expand from there.

Acolytes, Level 20 & Retirement

The Acolyte system unlocks at level 12. A character may have one Acolyte at a time.

  • When your current Acolyte becomes your main character, a new Acolyte can be created once that new main reaches level 12.
  • Retiring at level 20 passes 10% of the player's bank to the Acolyte. Retiring before level 20 passes 5%.
  • Main-character crafting materials and components do not automatically pass down.
  • Magic items passed to an Acolyte require DM approval.
  • An Acolyte only benefits from Bastion rooms/facilities appropriate to the Acolyte's current rank.
  • At level 20, post-20 advancement follows the current Legend/feat rules; Legend League contracts must explicitly allow level-20 play and do not give normal rewards.

Feedback, Bugs & Getting Help

Use the server feedback/reporting systems rather than letting issues disappear in chat.

  • /feedback can be used to praise a DM or submit player feedback/complaints.
  • Use the server's bug-reporting option for website/bot/system bugs.
  • DarQ serves as the player liaison for questions and feedback before escalation to Ooxy when appropriate.

Website Login & Access

The website uses Discord OAuth. Interactive systems are for Black Banner members; public/non-member access is limited to view-only areas.

  • Your Discord identity is mapped server-side for Gold, inventory, Bastions and other persistent systems.
  • Do not share login/session data or attempt to alter another player's ID in browser tools.
  • Admin controls are hidden from normal players and also protected by server-side permission checks.
