Wizard Spell Exchange

Copy a spell from another Wizard, or optionally trade one in return. The displayed costs are charged together only after the source player approves.

Request an exchange

Choose another Wizard within one rank. Each player learning a spell pays to copy it: normally 2 Work Hours + 50g per spell level. Applicable Savant features halve time and cost; Order of Scribes halves time only. These benefits do not stack. For a one-way copy, the source player contributes the same copying time but pays no transcription gold.

Nothing is reserved or spent while pending. Requests expire after 24 hours. The other player can approve here or with the green Discord button. Each player learning a spell adds it to their own character sheet; this tool does not edit D&D Beyond. Scroll copying is a separate procedure.
